SACT - Workshop Management

Module for complete workshop management: reception, diagnosis, repair and delivery. Replaces the standard repair flow with the states WAITING -> IN REPAIR -> READY -> DELIVERED / WRITTEN OFF.

Features

  • Service orders with timeline and notes.
  • Pre-delivery, delivery and write-off.
  • Purchase orders and supplies (internal cost).
  • License certificates with signatures.
  • Filtered reports and statistics by technician.
  • Printable documents with corporate header and WhatsApp QR code.
