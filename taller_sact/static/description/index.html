SACT - Computer Technical Service Workshop Management

Module for the comprehensive management of a computer technical service workshop: reception, diagnosis, repair and delivery. Replaces the standard repair flow with the statuses WAITING → IN REPAIR → READY → DELIVERED / WRITTEN OFF.

Features

  • Service orders with timeline and notes.
  • Pre-deliveries, deliveries and write-offs.
  • Spare-parts orders and supplies (internal cost).
  • License certificates with signatures.
  • Filtered reports and per-technician statistics.
  • Printables with corporate header and WhatsApp QR code.
