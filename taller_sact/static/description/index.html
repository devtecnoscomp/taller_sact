SACT - Sistema de Administración y Control de Taller

SACT (Sistema de Administración y Control de Taller) gestiona de forma integral un taller de servicio técnico de computación: recepción, diagnóstico, reparación y entrega de equipos. Reemplaza el flujo estándar de reparación de Odoo con un ciclo de vida propio: ESPERA → EN REPARACIÓN → LISTO → ENTREGADO / DE BAJA.

Key Features

  • Service orders with equipment data (type, brand, model, serial), customer, technician, accessories, charger, reported fault and work description.
  • Timeline / updates per order with notes and photos (chatter and attachments).
  • Pre-deliveries: diagnosis, previous solution, estimated price and a 9-digit code (barcode scannable).
  • Deliveries: applied solution, service quality, warranty, products/spare parts used, final price and exit date.
  • Write-offs: reason and observations (abandonment, not repairable, cancellation, duplicate, etc.).
  • Spare-parts orders with advance payment/balance control and pending/delivered status.
  • Supplies (spare parts, consumables, tools) with minimum stock, cost and low-stock alert.
  • On-site service jobs with technicians, amounts collected and expenses (food, fuel, materials) plus net amount.
  • License certificates with signatures, validity period and draft/issued/voided states.
  • Configurable catalogs: equipment types, brands, work types, license types and technicians.

Reports & Statistics

Filtered reports (deliveries by period, supplies, on-site jobs) and printables with corporate header and WhatsApp QR code. Includes per-technician statistics (orders, deliveries, revenue and percentages) and a public tracking portal for the customer via token (no login), with timeline and photos.
